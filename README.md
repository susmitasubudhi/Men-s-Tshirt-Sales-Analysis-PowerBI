# Men-s-Tshirt-Sales-Analysis-PowerBI|MYSQL
## Objectives
Analyze men's T-shirt sales data to evaluate brand performance, profitability, and pricing strategy using Power BI and MySQL.
## Business Problem
A fashion retail company wants to evaluate how pricing and discount strategies impact brand performance in the men's T-shirt Category .
While Higher discounts are expected to increase sales and revenue, management observed inconsistent revenue performance across brands.
The Objective of this analysis is to identify the key drivers of revenue by comparing discount levels, sales price, units sold and revenue efficiency across brands,
and to determine whether aggressive discount actually leads to higher revenue
## Business Questions
### 1. Does higher discount percentage lead to higher revenue?
### 2.Which brand generate maximum revenue and why>
### 3.How do pricing strategies affects units sold and revenue per units?
### 4.Which brand strategies perform better?
### 5.What pricing approach should the business focus on to maximize revenue?

## Analysis Approach
- Perform Brand wise analysis using ,pricing, discount, and sales metrics
- Calculated Avg Discount %, Avg Sales Price, Total Units sold, and total Revenue
-  Created Derived metrics revenue per unit to measure revenue efficiency
-  Compare Brand Psoitioning across volume-driven and premium strategies

## 📊 Key Insights
### Pricing vs Volume Strategy Finding
- Indian Garage Co drives customer demand through a wide product assortment and aggressive discounting, resulting in the highest sales volume.
- However, lower selling prices reduce revenue per unit.
- Premium brands like Armani Exchange achieve higher revenue efficiency through pricing power rather than product variety.
 ## Business Recommendations
 - Avoid excessive discounting as it reduces revenue efficiency.
 - Focus on optimizing pricing rather than maximizing units sold.
 - Maintain differentiated brand positioning between premium and value segments.
 - Use targeted discounts instead of blanket high discount strategies.

## Business Learning
- Higher product variety increases customer engagement and sales volume.
- However, assortment expansion alone does not guarantee higher revenue.
- Pricing strategy has a stronger impact on revenue generation than product variety.
